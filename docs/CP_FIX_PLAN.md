# CP fix plan, from the owner's scores (22 Nov 2026)

**Owner's score:** all six CP spots score **3 out of 5** on every criterion (shape and proportion, colour, brand boards, lighting, ground, scale). The gate is 4 or more. Nothing built so far has been compared side by side with a photo by the owner or by me; the six scores are the first outside measurement. Recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/CP_MATCH_LOG.md`. The plan below is the answer to the list I gave the owner on 22 Nov (six groups); it is ordered by what most plausibly moves a 3 to a 4, each phase small, looked at in the game at the six spots (`tools/cp_views.json`, day and night), logged, one pull request, and re-scored by the owner before the next phase starts when the owner wants to.
