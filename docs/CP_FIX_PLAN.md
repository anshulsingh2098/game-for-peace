# CP fix plan, from the owner's scores (22 Nov 2026)

**Owner's score:** all six CP spots score **3 out of 5** on every criterion (shape and proportion, colour, brand boards, lighting, ground, scale). The gate is 4 or more. Nothing built so far has been compared side by side with a photo by the owner or by me; the six scores are the first outside measurement. Recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/CP_MATCH_LOG.md`. The plan below is the answer to the list I gave the owner on 22 Nov (six groups); it is ordered by what most plausibly moves a 3 to a 4, each phase small, looked at in the game at the six spots (`tools/cp_views.json`, day and night), logged, one pull request, and re-scored by the owner before the next phase starts when the owner wants to.

| Phase | What | Why it moves the score | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 Facade realism** | Grime and soot under the cornices and on the ground floor (vertex colours, per building tone), air conditioner units and pipes on the upper floors, round shafts for the pavilion pilasters | shape, colour, scale: clean flat white reads as a model | three spots show the wear and the clutter; AC units counted per tile |

| **F4 Street life** | More traffic and parked cars at junctions (nose-in), people with more detail beyond the animated 18, more tree pictures, the roundabout island hedges, scooters | scale, shape | a junction frame with the density of a real one |

Rules: one phase a branch from main, the self-test must pass, no deploy without the owner's yes, every phase says in the log what was seen and what was not.

## Progress
- **F1 Facade realism, first pass done (22 Nov):** CP block walls get a per-edge tone (88 to 104 percent) and a soot fade over the top 8 m (down to 68 percent under the cornice); up to 7 air conditioner units (grey box with a dark grille) per wall of 8 m and more, on the first to third floors, and a drain pipe at one end of every CP wall (`tilemesh.js`, cap 420 units a tile); the pavilion pilasters are 8-sided round shafts (`cpfacade.js`). Seen in the game by day at three places: the tone changes and the faint darkening under the cornice are visible, one AC unit was seen; the effect is SUBTLE (the facade is still clean compared with real CP). Not done in F1: streaks under windows and sills (needs a texture pass), pipes and wires on the walls, the night look, frame cost, and the owner's re-score. Next: F1b (a stronger texture-based stain pass) if the owner agrees, then F2.
