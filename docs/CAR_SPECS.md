## What the game's physics can and cannot take (read from `src/vehicle/rapier-vehicle.js`)
- It models **peak power at a given rpm with a built-in curve shape** (`pt.kw`, `pt.peak`), not a measured torque curve. A real torque figure is used as a check (power from torque times rpm), not as an input. A turbo petrol V6 such as the GT-R is close to the built-in "turbo-like" shape; a diesel peaks early and a small petrol late.
- **Drive layout:** until now every car drove its rear wheels (the rally car's "four driven wheels" is only a sentence). A `drive` option (`'fwd'`, `'rwd'`, `'awd'`, default rear) has been added so the GT-Rs and the Endeavour put power through four wheels and the hatchbacks through the front. Existing cars are unchanged.
- **Gearbox:** a gear is never selected if the revs after the shift would fall under `pt.down` (default 0.6) of the limiter, so cars with wide ratio steps need a lower `down`.
- **Braking** is a deceleration number in the def (`brake`), limited by grip; **grip** comes from the Grip stat and the tyre model (Pacejka); **steering** from `steerMax` and the Handling stat; **shift time** (`pt.shiftTime`) is what makes a dual-clutch car feel quick (0.08 s) against a torque-converter automatic (0.22 s).
