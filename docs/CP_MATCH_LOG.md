## 2026-11-20  Driving feel, GTA research, road width, car proportion, road wear (owner: "roads not wide enough to turn", "match exact to GTA", "fix physics")
**GTA research (web, 19 Nov 2026).** No official or published lane width or vehicle dimension for GTA V exists in the sources I could reach (gtacars.net glossary, the eddlm handling guide, grandtheftwiki): the handling docs give `fSteeringLock` (inner wheel angle at standstill, shrinks with speed), grip in g (`fTractionCurveMax`), slip angle and masses, but no typical numbers and no sizes; press on GTA VI says GTA V's roads were deliberately exaggerated wide. So "exact GTA" is not possible from data; targets are the owner's frames, measured by eye (lane about 3.5 m, car about 52 to 54 percent of a lane).
**Physics measured** (`node tools/measure_turns.mjs car`, new; the real Rapier vehicle in a flat headless world): smallest circle at full lock was **6.4 m radius, a U-turn about 15 m wide**, while CP's roads are 9 to 13 m: a U-turn could never fit, and a tight corner needed the whole road. Cause: `steerMax` 0.50 rad (29 degrees) where real cars have 33 to 38 degrees. At 50 km/h the circle is about 11 m, set by the tyres and the yaw cap (`ARCADE.maxYaw` 1.2 rad/s caps the turn rate: 13.9 / 1.2 = 11.6 m, which matches); half lock gives 10 to 14 m at any speed (about what a player taps at a junction).
**Fixed**: `steerMax` 0.50 to 0.62 (Dusk GT): smallest circle 6.4 to 4.5 m radius, U-turn about 11 m. **Tried and rejected**: raising `maxYaw` to 1.5 to 2.0: a sustained full-lock circle at 40 to 80 km/h then collapses into a spin (speed falls to 13 to 30 km/h, 4 m radius), which is not GTA-like either; the yaw cap is left at 1.2. Not fixed, not measured by a person: the feel at speed. The sustained-full-lock test is not a good proxy for how a player turns (it saturates the yaw assist); a driven lap by a person is the real check.
**Car in lane**: car drawn at 0.88 (about 4.26 x 2.13 x 1.40 m with mirrors) and tertiary lanes 3.5 m, secondary 3.6, primary and trunk 3.7, residential 3.1 (`pack.js`, `packio.mjs`): the car is about 61 percent of a tertiary lane with mirrors, about 56 with the body alone, against 52 to 54 in the GTA frames. Audit: footways inside carriageways 94 to 120 (the baseline was regenerated again, still flagged for Anshul's Claude).
**Roads wider**: CP circles drawn at least 12 m (Inner) and 16 m (Outer), from the data's 9 and 13 (`CP_WIDTH` in `smooth.js`; an estimate from the GTA frames and the several lanes each way of the real circles, NOT a survey).
**Road detail**: new `wearDetail` in `tilemesh.js`: worn dark wheel tracks in every lane, tar patches (dark and lighter), manhole covers, hairline cracks, on trunk to residential streets of 5.5 m and wider, in the marking layer, with a per-tile budget (7,000 / 500 / 260 / 360). `?nowear` turns it off. Seen on the Inner Circle: tracks and patches read like the GTA frames' worn asphalt. Cost (Connaught Place start, q=med, bench 30, visible pane, desktop Mac): 400 draw calls, 321 k triangles, p95 11.2 ms, JS heap 411 MB (fresh tab; the 958 MB seen earlier was an old tab). Not done: signs on poles (street name blades, one-way and speed signs: GTA frames show them), tyre marks near junctions, patch lines along kerbs, drain grates, a phone run.
Checks: self-test 116 checks; vehicle acceptance no problems.
