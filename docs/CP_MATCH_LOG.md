Comparison (overhead, visible pane): Inner Circle at x 1044, z -1975 now has several dashed lane lines along its whole curve; the signal junction at x 794, z -2263 shows zebra crossings drawn in full (the earlier lift fix confirmed by eye) with stop lines beside them. Verdict: **close**. Not verified: that each stop line is on the side the cars really arrive on (the side rule is read from the traffic code, not watched with cars driving up to it); stop lines at the Inner Circle entries (those nodes are not signals in the data, so none is drawn there); line widths and the dash length are the game's old values, not measured from the photos; no scripted drive yet.

## 2026-11-19  Road markings vanishing near the car (owner report with screenshot)
Report: lane dashes and the zebra crossing in front of the car were partly or fully missing, and there were no markings under or around the car; markings seemed to appear only far ahead and disappear as the car came closer.
Cause (read in `tilemesh.js`): the road surface is lifted 5 cm over the ground (`LIFT.road`), but the zebra stripes were drawn at height 0 and the lane dashes at the road's own height without the marking lift (`LIFT.mark`, 7 cm), so both lay UNDER the asphalt and only a depth offset (polygon offset) pulled them forward. That offset is enough far away (the depth buffer is coarse there) and not near the camera (the buffer is fine there, and the near plane is 0.3 m in chase views), so markings vanished close to the car and in front of it. The footway crossing bars already had the lift, so they were not affected. Not caused by the CP changes of this session.
Fix: zebra stripes at `gy + LIFT.mark`; lane dashes at `... + LIFT.mark`.
Comparison: before (owner screenshot): no dashes within about 10 m of the car, zebra half drawn. After (autodrive at the Connaught Place start, q=med): dashes appear beside and just ahead of the car at 50 km/h. Verdict: **fixed for lane dashes (seen)**. The zebra crossing itself was not seen again after the fix (the drive did not pass one in my views); same change as the dashes, so expected fixed, **not yet seen**. Note: road roughly under the car at a junction has no markings by design (no marks within 9 m of a real node), and the Inner Circle has none at all because it is tagged without lanes (tertiary, 9 m, no lane count): to be decided with the photos, which show lane lines on the circles' carriageway only at the entries.

## 2026-11-19  Ground: stone slab paving (element: paving)
References: R2, R3, R4 (and R1 for the kerb edge): wide, flat, warm grey-cream stone slabs, thin joints, a slightly polished sheen, a tactile strip along the walk, a clean kerb edge; no bare earth. What the photos do NOT settle: exact slab size (R2 and R4 suggest larger than 1.2 m, perhaps 1.5 to 2 m) and the pattern of the inlay lines.
Built: new `plaza` mesh and material in `src/world/city/tilemesh.js` (`makeMaterials`), listed in `cityworld.js`: a procedural canvas texture, 4 by 4 slabs of 1.2 m (one repeat is 4.8 m), cream with per-slab tone, speckle, hairline cracks and a dark joint, roughness 0.6 (a faint sheen); used for the ground of every block whose centre is within 345 m of the CP centre (`CP_CIRCLE.PLAZA_R` in `smooth.js`) and whose area is `cp`; before, those blocks were drawn with the sand texture. The yard paving of P1 part 3 is switched off for `cp` (the blocks are paved already).
Comparison (overhead at x 1150, z -2030 and street level at x 970, z -2225, visible pane): the bare orange earth is gone; the first version was nearly white (too bright against R2 and R4), tinted down to a warm grey-cream (`0xc9c0aa`). Verdict: **close**. Still different: no tactile strip along the walk, no real reflection (the photos show wet-look polished stone; here only a faint sheen), slab size is a guess, no inlay lines, the kerb at the plaza edge is the plain pavement kerb, the same texture also covers the arcade walk under the columns (the columns' own walk is the old pavers and should be changed to match).
Cost (Connaught Place start, q=med, bench 30, visible pane, desktop Mac): draw calls 383 to 385, triangles about 315 k (the plaza adds ground polygons only), p95 16.3 ms. JS heap read 958 MB in that run against 270 to 372 MB earlier: the tab had been reloaded many times, so this is NOT yet a finding; to be re-measured in a fresh tab.
Licence: the texture is drawn in code, own work, no outside image. Recorded in `assets/ASSETS.md`.
