## 2026-11-20  Driving feel, GTA research, road width, car proportion, road wear (owner: "roads not wide enough to turn", "match exact to GTA", "fix physics")
**GTA research (web, 19 Nov 2026).** No official or published lane width or vehicle dimension for GTA V exists in the sources I could reach (gtacars.net glossary, the eddlm handling guide, grandtheftwiki): the handling docs give `fSteeringLock` (inner wheel angle at standstill, shrinks with speed), grip in g (`fTractionCurveMax`), slip angle and masses, but no typical numbers and no sizes; press on GTA VI says GTA V's roads were deliberately exaggerated wide. The sustained-full-lock test is not a good proxy for how a player turns (it saturates the yaw assist); a driven lap by a person is the real check.
