# Kartavya Path and Raisina Hill: the owner's reference photos (descriptions only; photos stay outside the repository)

Owner, 21 Nov 2026: five photos covering the overall design, structure, day and night look, road, side paths, poles and chains, and a view of the North and South Blocks. Registered as K1 to K5 (CP photos are R7 and R8 in `docs/CP_REFERENCES.md`). Measured by eye, error about 15 percent. Atlas places: DEL-006 India Gate, DEL-007 Kartavya Path, DEL-008 Rashtrapati Bhavan (`docs/CP_MAX_CHECKLIST.md`, scope SUR).

## K1. Aerial by day, from above India Gate looking west (overall structure)
- A dead straight axis of about 2.3 km from India Gate to Rashtrapati Bhavan, symmetrical. **India Gate** (sandstone arch, 42 m) stands inside a **round paved plaza** with a ring road; in front of it, on the axis, **a canopy (chhatri) standing in a rectangular pool** with a lawn panel on each side.
- West of the Gate the axis is: a **central carriageway** (dark asphalt, two lanes each way, kerbed in **red sandstone**), then on each side a **lawn panel** in a long rectangle, then a **double row of tall trees** (dense, dark green), then a long narrow **canal** (water channel about 12 m wide), then more trees and lawn. The cross streets pass over the axis at right angles every 450 m or so with a **bridge over each canal**; at each crossing the lawns end and a paved crossing joins the two sides.
- At the far end, on a rise, the **Secretariat blocks (North and South)** each with a dome and a smaller dome, and **Rashtrapati Bhavan** with its big dome between them.
- Colours: buff and red sandstone, green lawn, deep green trees, olive water, grey road; clear blue sky with haze at the horizon.
## K2. Day, on a red sandstone path over a canal toward the North/South Block
- A **flat red-pink sandstone path about 6 m wide** bordered by pale stone, crossing the canal with a **balustrade of sandstone piers every 1.6 m joined by a plain stepped top rail** (low, about 0.9 m). The canal water is green. A person walks on it (1.7 m): the balustrade reaches mid-thigh.
- Beyond, a **long Secretariat block**: buff stone, plain flat wall with tall narrow windows in vertical bands, a **projecting centre with a dome** (low cream dome with a ring of small arched openings under it and a finial), roof line flat with small pavilions. Low red sandstone **wall (about 1.5 m)** along the canal side with a square gate pier.
- Black lamp post with a lantern head, round-canopy trees (neem, jamun type) and a flowering tree, lawn right and left.
## K3. Raisina Hill road between the Blocks, from the road (view toward Vijay Chowk)
- A **wide plain carriageway** (about 30 m, no marking except **one solid yellow centre line**) running between two **red sandstone retaining walls** (about 3 m high, stepped) that end in **chhatris**: a **square-plan kiosk on four pairs of slender columns with a copper-gold dome** and a small finial, about 5 m high, one on each side exactly facing the other. Beyond, the axis continues into a green avenue and the far skyline.
- Colours: pink-red sandstone, grey asphalt, yellow line, pale blue sky.
## K4. Roadside of Kartavya Path: sandstone bollards and chains, lamps, kerb
- **Footpath of pink sandstone slabs** about 4 m wide with a **cream stone border**; along its road edge **short square bollards of cream stone (about 0.8 m, rounded caps) about 2.5 m apart with black chains hanging between them in swags**; the road edge has a **solid white line** and a **dashed white lane line**; the carriageway is dark grey asphalt.
- **Black classic lamp posts** (about 5 m, a fluted base, a lantern at the top, a bracket for flags or a second light) every 25 to 30 m; a low sandstone-coloured wall and **trimmed hedge** behind; a **green DTC low-floor bus** on the road; the North Block with a dome far behind; trees with round crowns. Lawn on the near side, short and bright green.
## K5. Night aerial from India Gate looking west up Kartavya Path
- The whole axis lit by **two rows of amber lamps** in perspective (a lamp about every 25 m on each side), the central promenade packed with people, **lit trees** (amber from below), **canals lit blue-violet** at the near crossing, dark lawns, **orange folding barricades** along the crossing roads, **traffic streaks** (red and yellow) at the cross streets, a few buses, and Rashtrapati Bhavan and the Blocks **lit in pink, green and purple** far down the axis; cranes and a glass tower at the right; heavy grey evening cloud.
- The central road is closed to cars in the evening and used as a promenade (the white lines still show on it).

## What the game has now (21 Nov build, Kartavya zone from PR 110)
One carriageway with the markings and red/yellow painted kerb; the avenue's lawns and lamps and a hedge; palm cards; the landmark models (India Gate arch, Rashtrapati Bhavan, the Blocks) from `assets/cities/delhi/landmarks.json`.
