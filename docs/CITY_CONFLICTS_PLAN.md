# Plan: rails, roads, buildings and heights that conflict (owner report, 18 Nov 2026)

## The report
A screenshot of a city covered in long parallel dark strips with white lines, trains on them and the old low-poly trees; "what are these rails across the city", "a mess in the rail system and network", rails overlapping roads, buildings overlapping roads, places where the car sinks, and places where it sinks and falls into a tunnel. Wanted: a phased fix.

## What was checked (Rajul's Claude, 18 Nov 2026)
- The striped view was **not reproduced** in the live game (`c085cc44`) at Connaught Place, New Delhi station (the yard shows service roads and the station kit) or the India-map start at Mathura (real tracks and a station, clean). The screenshot has the old generated trees, so it is from an older build or another place. **Needed from the owner for each defect: the coordinate line** (menu, bottom line, "Copy": latitude, longitude, game x and z, the street) and the build address.
- Where rails come from: the city pack has no rail class in its street graph (classes: motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, residential, service, footway, cycleway, busway). Delhi's railway stations are generated kits (`src/world/city/transit_rail.js`: platforms, tracks in the gaps, a rake on every third track; "the tracks are not joined"). The Metro is `transit.json` (lines, stations). The India map has its own rail network (`rails.json`, `src/world/rail_view.js`, `rail_control*.js`). So three separate rail systems exist that do not share data, which is why they disagree.
- Known conflict counts in the pack (`manifest.json`): 46,980 street pairs touching, 5,135 streets dropped, 311 footprints dropped as overlapping, 165 dropped as too small. Heights: flyover decks and underpasses are separate height functions (`deckHeight`, `underDepth` in `src/world/city/pack.js`) with ground-layer steps (PR 79); the car physics picks deck or ground by comparing heights (`main.js` `world.height`).
- Likely causes of the sinking and tunnel falls (to confirm with coordinates): a sunken street (layer below 0) whose ramp ends do not match its neighbours, so `underDepth` jumps; an open-cut hole in the ground over a tunnel that has no floor at the entrance; the deck/ground choice flipping at a flyover foot.

## Todarmal Road, checked 18 Nov 2026 (owner: "check near Todarmal Road, residential")
**Reproduced.** Todarmal Road runs at game x 2190 to 2470, z about -1640 (lat 28.63, lon 77.23, near Bengali Market and Tilak Bridge). From the air at x 2290, z -1760 the live game (`c085cc44`) shows exactly the owner's picture: a fan of long parallel dark strips with white lane lines, a blue car on one of them, running under and between large buildings. Facts from the pack (`node tools/audit_city_area.mjs 2290 -1760 130`):
- 66 street edges within 130 m of that point: 48 are **unnamed two-lane two-way residential** edges, 9 tertiary one-way, 5 named Todarmal Road, 4 Tansen Marg, 8 Babar Road. So the strips are not rails: the builder has them as ordinary residential streets (no layer, no ramp), so they are drawn with lane marks and driven on.
- In an 800 x 600 m box around Todarmal Road, 73 of 176 buildings (41 percent) touch a street corridor (half width plus 1.5 m); the worst intrude by 3 to 4.3 m (a 12.8 or 16 m block at x 2369, z -1691). Only one layered street (a footway) is in the box, so flyover and underpass height data is not involved here: the car sinking and the tunnel fall are elsewhere, or come from the deck and ground choice in `world.height`.
- The same pattern exists elsewhere (a crude test, unnamed straight residential edges of 80 m or more with three or more parallel siblings within 14 m, finds 335 edges, in clusters around x -15000, z -21500 and x -1500, z -4000); the Todarmal fan is made of shorter edges, so that test misses it: a proper detector is part of C0.
**Likely cause (not confirmed: the OSM tags could not be fetched, the Overpass servers timed out):** a railway corridor or yard near Tilak Bridge and the New Delhi approach tagged `highway=residential` (or lanes of unnamed service roads) by mappers, or the builder splitting a rail yard's `railway=*` ways into road edges. Check by looking at the OSM tags of those ways (`way(28.6288,77.2315,28.6305,77.2335)[highway=residential]`) and by diffing against the rail tracks of the India network at the same place.
**First fix that is safe now (C2/C3 start):** a generation rule in `rules.js`, `street_fan`: an unnamed residential or service edge that has 3 or more near-parallel unnamed siblings within 14 m and runs through a building is not drawn and not routed (a class of error fixed everywhere, like `street_building`), with the audit counting it and a screenshot check at Todarmal Road before and after. Not applied yet: the detector must first handle short edges.

## Update, 18 Nov 2026 (after "fix strips"): two different things look like strips
1. **Station track kits (fixed in part, live `eb901661`, branch `fix/delhi-green`, PR 96).** `src/world/city/transit_rail.js` drew every station's tracks 220 m past the platforms on both sides, as dark ballast strips with light rails that read as roads with lane lines. Tilak Bridge has 7 platform faces in the data (the real station has 2 platforms, 4 tracks; OpenStreetMap shows a band of about 12 parallel tracks there, the New Delhi to Nizamuddin corridor), so it draws a fan. Now: tails of 45 m instead of 220 m, rake travel 120 m instead of 260 m, ballast in warm gravel and darker rails. Still wrong: the kit is not clipped against streets and buildings, has no level crossings, and its track count comes from platform faces, not from the real tracks (C3, C4).
2. **A fan of unnamed two-lane residential street edges** near Todarmal Road (48 within 130 m, mostly 2-point straight edges of 35 to 70 m at about -57 degrees, plus others at other angles). They are still drawn as roads and still carry cars. They are **not** inside railway land-use areas (only 2 of them are), they are mostly **not** through buildings, and a parallel-siblings detector (3 siblings within 14 m, then 2 within 30 m, through a building 8 m) finds none of them (the rule was written, tested on the whole pack, found 485 then 91 edges and none at Todarmal, and was **not shipped**). So the data may be correct small lanes drawn too wide and too regular, or an OSM tagging problem: the OSM tags of those ways are still unchecked (the Overpass servers time out). Next: C0 must write the edge ids of what is on screen (a debug overlay: click a street, show its OSM id and tags) before any rule is written.

## Phases (each: a measurable check first, one fix, a regression test, a PR, a log entry)
**C0, Measure (no game change).** A tool `tools/audit_city.mjs` that reads the pack and prints, with coordinates: (a) street pairs of the same layer that cross without a node (road-road); (b) building footprints overlapping a street corridor (width by class); (c) layered edges whose height profile jumps more than 0.3 m between neighbours, and nodes where the layer changes with no ramp; (d) sunken edges with no matching ramp or whose open cut has no floor; (e) rail items (station tracks, Metro lines, the India rail network where it enters the city) crossing a street at the same height with no crossing flag. Output: counts per class and a list of the 50 worst spots as `at=` links. Done when the report exists and the owner's defects appear in it.
**C1, Heights and falls (safety first).** One surface function `surfaceAt(x, z, yHint)` for the car and the player, with a continuity test at every layered edge; fix ramps found by C0(c, d); a safety net in the physics: below the surface by more than 2 m with no tunnel under it, put the car back on the nearest street (the "To road" action, automatic). Done when the scripted street-driving test (P2, B-list) drives 150 streets with no sink and no fall, and a unit test builds each ramp type.
**C2, Buildings against roads.** Roads win: clip or drop a footprint that overlaps a street corridor (corridor = carriageway + sidewalk by class); keep landmarks, shrink the footprint instead. Done when C0(b) reports under 1 percent of the present count and a screenshot check at 8 places shows no building on a road.
**C3, One rail model.** Decide the single source of truth: the India rail network (`rails.json`) for main lines and yards, `transit.json` for Metro, the station kits only as the platform detail on the real tracks. Remove duplicates and yard sidings that are drawn as roads or parallel stripes; draw only main and Metro tracks plus the real yard tracks once. Done when the rail count in the city is stated, every track belongs to one line or yard, and no two tracks lie within 1 m.
**C4, Road and rail crossings.** For every at-grade crossing: a level-crossing (barrier, signal, the road has the right of way only when the barrier is up) or a bridge or underpass layer; none left as plain overlap. Done when C0(e) reports none unflagged.
**C5, Pack data fixes.** The builder (`tools/build_city.mjs`): the 46,980 touching pairs and 5,135 dropped streets, the layer inference for bridges and tunnels from OSM (`bridge`, `tunnel`, `layer` tags), rail kept as its own list. Needs the 225 MB OSM extract (the owner's yes). Done when the pack's conflict counts fall and `tools/validate_city.mjs` has the new checks.
**C6, Regression.** The audit runs in `tools/daily_check.py` (counts must not rise) and the street-driving test runs in the self-test where it can.
