# CP reference photos from the owner (descriptions only; the photos stay outside the repository)

Owner, 21 Nov 2026. Two new Connaught Place references. What each shows, measured by eye (error about 15 percent, the photos have no scale mark except the walking person), and what the current game does differently. Registered here, not in `docs/CP_MATCH_LOG.md`, so this file cannot clash with the stacked PRs that edit that log; copy the lines across after they merge.

## R7. A CP block corner, from street level (daylight, low angle)
**Shows.** The corner of a block where two arcade fronts meet at an angle. White to cream weathered stucco, flat, no colour except the shop boards.
- **Ground floor:** an arcade. At the corner two large **smooth round columns** (no flutes visible), standing close together as a pair, with a plain square base block and a plain capital under a deep flat entablature. Behind them, set back, **round-arched openings** with a keystone, between flat pilasters; on the sides the arcade continues with single columns spaced about 3 column diameters apart.
- **First floor:** a plain wall face at the corner; **arched windows with a semicircular fanlight (radial bars)** set in a recessed panel, a plain square window below it; small balcony-like sills; the board letters stand on the wall above the arcade.
- **Roof corner:** NOT a dome. A **square tower** (a pavilion) rises above the roof line at the corner: two tall slender free-standing **round columns** on each visible face (a pair at the corner, a further pair at the side) carrying a flat cornice, a stepped parapet with a **balustrade** along the roof line on both fronts. Its top is flat with a thin cornice slab.
- **Scale cue:** a walking person in the right half is about 1.7 m: the ground-floor columns are about three times the person (about 5 m), the first floor about the same again, the roof tower about 4 m more.
- **Ground:** large grey stone slabs, wet or polished, long shadow from the colonnade; a granite block bench and a wooden barrel litter bin in the foreground; thin black railing at the left.
- **Boards:** red fascia boards (one slanted over the arcade corner, white capitals), a blue horizontal board high on the right front, small red signs on columns; all lettering plain white or red capitals on a long board.
**Game now (21 Nov build).** The corner is a round drum with a collar, a two-tier dome and a finial (`drumCorner` in `cpfacade.js`). That does not match this photo: the real corner pavilion is square with paired columns, a flat cornice and a balustrade, no dome.
**To build.** Square pavilion (about 6 m a side) above the corner with two paired round columns per face (about 0.45 m across, 4 m tall), entablature slab, balustrade along the parapet on both fronts; fanlight arched window in a recessed panel below; round column pair at the corner of the arcade (about 0.6 m across, 5 m tall) with plain base and capital; round-arched openings with keystone behind. Check at the spot with `tools/cp_views.json`.

## R8. CP at night, from the roof of Central Park's side (high oblique, warm light)
**Shows.** The Inner Circle curving across the picture with the blocks in a continuous arc, each front washed in **warm yellow-white light from under and along the arcade**, so the whole colonnade glows; the facades above are lit white by wall washers. Between the blocks a **radial road** runs away from the circle with streaks of traffic (red tail lights, yellow head lights), a signal head at the lower left, **zebra crossings** across each radial mouth, and **amber street lamps**. Trees are lit from below in orange and green. A **tricolour flag** on a tall pole at the left (the Central Park flag). Windows above the arcade show a few lit squares, most are dark. The park centre is dark.
**Game now.** Warm soffit lights, glowing lamp heads and light pools exist (PR 103, 105 to 111: lights on columns and walls, wet reflections at night); the wall-wash on the first floor and the lit trees do not exist; no flag pole; traffic streaks are not drawn.
**To build.** A white-yellow wall wash on the first-floor face of the arcade blocks at night (additive, cheap, distance limited); lit windows on a small share of upper floors; warm under-lighting of the lowest tree canopies on the walks; the Central Park flag pole and flag (about 40 m, a cloth that moves); traffic light streaks only as a night glow on the lamps of the cars. Check at the night spot.
