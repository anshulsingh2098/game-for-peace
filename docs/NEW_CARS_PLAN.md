# Plan: seven new cars, paint variants, carjacking and damage (owner request, 19 Nov 2026)

## The request
Seven cars supplied in `~/Downloads/CAR ASSETS/` to be added as playable cars like the existing ones, in several colours, at the right scale and proportion; the player can pull a driver out of a car, throw them on the road and take the car (GTA V style); and all the damage and physics logic applies to the new cars too.

## What the game expects of a car (read from `src/vehicle/vehicle.js`, `defs.js`, `paint.js`)
Model space: front +Z, up +Y, +X is the car's left, metres. Four empty nodes `Wheel_FL`, `Wheel_FR`, `Wheel_RL`, `Wheel_RR` at the wheel centres carry the wheel meshes (they spin and steer). A def in `src/vehicle/defs.js` gives mass, wheel radius, track, power train numbers, lights, camera and a `paint` block (named paint materials and a colour list). Existing cars are 0.4 to 0.9 MB (the concept car 3 MB). **Budget (proposed): 70,000 triangles and 4 MB a car at most, loaded only when the car is chosen.**

## Phases (each: a measurable check, one change, a PR, a log entry)
**C0 Conversion pipeline (no game change).** `tools/make_car_glb.py` (Blender, headless): one recipe per car (parts to keep, real length, wheels, paint material, texture size, decimation). Output `assets/cars/<id>.glb` plus a short preview picture. Done when each car loads, has four wheel nodes and a named paint material, and is under budget. The SX4 and Everest first (clean rigs), then GT-R50 and R35, then the Maruti 800, last the Figo and Aspire (the wheels have to be cut out).
**C1 Cars in the garage.** A `defs.js` entry for each (mass, wheel radius, track, engine data from published specs, camera, lights, stats), six or more paint colours, ride height and scale checked against the real size (see the scale rule: wheel radius, wheelbase and length within 3 percent of the real car). Done when each drives, the wheels turn and steer, and a screenshot at the same camera as the Dusk GT shows the right size next to it.
**C1b The cars as NPC traffic (owner, 19 Nov 2026: "not just in the garage but other NPC cars too").** Delhi's ambient traffic (`citytraffic.js` + `vehicles.js` `Fleet`) draws box cars, one instanced mesh per kind (hatch, suv, taxi, ...). Each new car also gets a low-polygon version (about 3,000 triangles, `tools/blender/make_traffic_cars.py`) stored in the traffic pack (`tools/merge_traffic_pack.py` adds it without touching the existing thirteen), and `MODEL_SETS` in `vehicles.js` says which base kind it stands in for and for what share of the cars (the Maruti 800 and Figo are common on a Delhi road, the GT-Rs are rare). Until the pack loads, and if it fails, every car stays a box. Parked cars use the same models.
**C2 Carjacking.** On foot, near a traffic or parked car with a driver: a prompt, the driver is pulled out (the animation of `exitseq.js` and the ragdoll that PR 131 added), thrown onto the road, the player gets in and the car becomes the player's, with the traffic system giving the car up cleanly. Needs the traffic cars (`citytraffic.js`, `traffic_pack.glb`) to carry drivers and to be swappable for one of these seven cars: so the seven cars also join the traffic, not just the garage.
**C3 Damage and physics on the new cars.** Follows Anshul's `docs/DAMAGE_PLAN.md` (branch `vehicle/damage-1`): doors, bonnet, boot and bumpers are separate meshes on the SX4 and Everest, so they can dent, hang and fall off; the other cars need the same split. Done when the same hit test gives the same result on all seven.
**C4 Regression.** Self-test, the Delhi QA drive runner (`docs/DELHI_TESTING.md`) and a frame-cost measure of each car on the desktop and, with the owner's help, on the iPhone.

## Open questions for the owner (nothing blocks C0)
1. **Licence and brand.** None of the folders holds a licence file. These look like downloaded models of real cars (Nissan, Ford, Suzuki, Maruti) with real badges. The repo's own cars have invented names. Decide: keep the real names and badges, or rename and blank the badges before the game is deployed to the public. Until then each car is listed in `assets/ASSETS.md` as "owner supplied, licence unknown".
2. **Download budget.** Seven cars at up to 4 MB is up to 28 MB, loaded one at a time; the Lite bundle stays as it is.
3. **Phone.** The iPhone 14 Pro run was 43.5 fps average on the live build; seven heavier cars may need the lower tier to use fewer triangles.

## Status, 19 Nov 2026 (Stream A, Rajul's session)
Built and seen in the game (desktop pane, `q=med`): **Suzuki SX4** and **Ford Endeavour (Everest)** in the garage (`assets/cars/sx4.glb` 68k triangles 3.5 MB, `everest.glb` 110k triangles 3.1 MB; real specs in `defs.js`; seven colours each; paint works; the self-test passes, the acceptance script reports no problems: SX4 0 to 100 km/h in 9.7 s and 187 km/h, Endeavour 10.4 s and 179 km/h) and in Delhi NPC traffic (17 Endeavours and 14 SX4s counted in a 20-second sample). Lessons kept in the scripts: the FBX leaves most materials in alpha-blend mode (wheels and panels look see-through and mottled until made opaque); this game's gearbox never shifts into a gear whose revs fall under 60 percent of the limiter, so a car needs six close ratios or a lower `down` setting. **Added later the same day:** the **GT-R50** and the **GT-R R35 LB Works** (`gtr50.glb` 80k triangles 2.6 MB, `r35.glb` 56k triangles 2.0 MB), real specs and all-wheel drive in `defs.js` (R35 0 to 100 in 3.0 s against 2.7 claimed, 320 km/h; GT-R50 2.7 s, 320 km/h; dual-clutch shift time 0.07 to 0.08 s, the premium feel lever), the R35 also as a rare NPC (one hatch in eighty); the supplied R35 livery (a tiger decal part) was removed. Figures and sources in `docs/CAR_SPECS.md`. **Not done:** the Maruti 800, Figo and Aspire (recipes still to write; the Figo and Aspire have to have their wheels cut out); the Endeavour is over the triangle budget (110k against 70k); the traffic versions lose their glass at 3,000 triangles; carjacking (C2); damage on the new cars (C3, waits for Anshul's `vehicle/damage-1`); phone frame cost; no licence file for any of the seven.
