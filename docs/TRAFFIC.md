# Indian traffic (N12): what is built and what is not

Code: `src/traffic/sim.js` (simulation, pure), `src/traffic/traffic.js` (drawing), region data `traffic.mix` and `traffic.scale` (`regions.js`). Tests: two checks added in `tools/selftest.mjs` (55 in all) on top of the earlier traffic tests (lanes, sides, spawn gaps, determinism, collision, autodrive).

## Built
- **Vehicle mix from region data.** `trafficKinds(pack, mix)`: the thirteen pack vehicles keep their relative weights and together stand for the mix's car, van, truck and bus share; `two-wheeler` and `auto-rickshaw` are added in proportion to the mix (Mumbai: 20 and 14 of 100). A place that is not a region has no mix and keeps the pack as it is.
- **Two new vehicle types**, drawn from original boxes until there are real models (`boxGeometry` in `traffic.js`): a motorbike with its rider and a three-wheeler auto-rickshaw (yellow body, black hood). Two-wheelers ride at the edge of their lane (their lateral offset is a fixed 0.2 to 1.1 m from the lane centre), are smaller obstacle circles, and otherwise follow the same lane rules (gap keeping, stopping for the player, stuck removal).
- **Traffic signals.** At the navigation graph's signalled crossings (boulevards and highways) vehicles obey a 40 s cycle (green 24 s, amber 3 s, red 13 s, offset per crossing): on red, or on amber when they cannot stop, they brake at 3 m/s² to stop 1.5 m before the stop line; they do not enter on red unless already too close to stop (tested: a few percent). Pedestrians (N13) cross on red.
- Density is region data (`traffic.scale` multiplies Off, Low, Mid and High), spawning is deterministic, the active cap is the mode's, recovery of stuck cars exists (12 s).

## Not built (N12 asks for these)
- **Intersections and turns.** There are no junctions in the generated world yet, so traffic only follows lanes; routing over the navigation graph, turning and yielding wait for N11's junction and local street. The sim still drives by the road lookup, not by `NavGraph.route` (it would give the same result on a single road).
- **Overtaking and weaving** by two-wheelers beyond their lane-edge position, horns, indicators.
- Real models for two-wheelers and rickshaws, and engine and horn sounds for them (the asset job, `docs/ASSET_PLAN.md` to be written).
- A phone measurement of the extra draw calls (two more instanced meshes at most).

**City buses and cows (19 Nov 2026).** `bus` (10.5 m, red body, cream band, 0.85 of road speed) and `cow` (1.9 m, 0.1 of road speed, about 3 m/s, no lights) are extra kinds like the two-wheeler and the auto-rickshaw: a region's traffic mix may list them (Delhi, Agra and Bhopal have cows; most have buses). Cars do not overtake, so a cow in a lane holds up the cars behind it (the self-test checks the queue). The bus is no longer counted in the share of the model pack. Procedural shapes, not real models.

## Indian driving rules in the traffic (19 Nov 2026)
Sources: the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways speed notification of April 2018 (cars 120 km/h on expressways, 100 on national highways, 70 in urban areas; goods vehicles 80; two-wheelers 80 on highways) and the Central Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations 2017 (keep left; the driver being overtaken keeps left; heavy or speed-restricted vehicles use the left lane of a multi-lane carriageway except when overtaking; overtaking is on the right). Figures for buses and for urban two-wheeler limits differ between sources and are approximate. Not verified from the primary text: priority at roundabouts and at uncontrolled junctions (so no such rule is built).
What is built (`traffic/sim.js`): traffic keeps left; on roads with more than one lane each way slow and heavy kinds (trucks, buses, autos, two-wheelers, cows) spawn in and keep to the left (outermost) lane; a car blocked by a slower vehicle moves to the lane towards the median to overtake, and returns to the left lane when the gap is clear (no undertaking); a lane change needs room for a three-second closing gap ahead and a margin behind, is not made near a junction, takes about two seconds and is followed by a 4 s cool-down; speed caps by class (`KIND_CAP`: trucks and goods vehicles 80 km/h, buses 90, two-wheelers 80, auto-rickshaws about 50; cars take the road's limit). The self-test runs a three-lane road with cows, trucks, buses and cars and checks: slow kinds spawn left, cars overtake on the right and come back, cows stay left, no two vehicles overlap (also while changing lane), caps hold, and cars are not stuck behind cows.
Not built: overtaking on single-lane-each-way roads (it needs the oncoming lane), indicators and horns, two-wheelers weaving between lanes, wrong-way riders, lane-less behaviour in dense city traffic, priority rules at roundabouts and uncontrolled junctions, police checkposts, and per-class speed limits in urban zones (cars use the road style's limit).
